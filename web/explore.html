What these estimates include

Walking, waiting, riding and transfers are part of elapsed time. Earliness is separate. TRAX excludes S-Line and FrontRunner. Bus + TRAX checks bus and rail alternatives separately too; it is still a bounded search.

Bike uses a maximum flat-ground speed of 4.17 m/s and directional elevation. Missing elevation stays unknown. Times are not rider-calibrated. No reliability or return-trip estimate.

The area guide checks five origins per cell, up to 250 m apart. Mixed cells remain unknown. It cannot resolve every barrier or address: select a point to check it. The contour study is smaller than the loaded street extract.
